About Us

EggX is a digital marketplace for fresh eggs that connects poultry farmers across the country with a vast network of fresh egg consumers in their areas, where they can sell their farm eggs at the best prices they want.

Recognizing the growing importance of digital technologies in transforming traditional industries, our company is committed to improving farmer livelihoods through innovation and digital transformation. Leveraging its expertise in animal healthcare, supply chain management, and information technology, the company has developed the FARMEX ecosystem, within which the EggX mobile application has been created.

EggX is a fintech-enabled digital commodity trading platform designed to connect poultry farmers directly with retailers through a transparent and efficient marketplace. By eliminating the traditional role of commission agents (Arhtis), the application helps farmers secure better prices while enabling retailers to purchase fresh eggs at competitive farm-gate rates.

The company has integrated advanced technologies, including AI-powered route optimization, digital payments, automated trade matching, logistics management, and full transaction traceability, to enhance market efficiency and reduce operational costs. Through EggX, farmers, retailers, and transporters are connected on a single digital platform that streamlines trading, logistics, and payments.

By developing EggX, the company is driving the adoption of Agriculture 4.0 in Pakistan, promoting transparency, reducing wastage, improving supply chain efficiency, and creating a more sustainable and profitable agricultural ecosystem.

Key Features:

1: Real-time Bids and Auctions: The app promotes market transparency and accurate price trends through real-time auction bids on offers to protect farmers and egg consumers from future losses.

2: Transparent Pricing: Monitor market trends and price changes to ensure fair and competitive transactions.

3: Verified Genuine Buyers::Opportunity to connect directly with verified consumers, eliminating the hassle of marketing.

4: Efficient Transaction Management: The app offers an automated logistics system so that farmers and buyers can continue their business activities without getting bogged down in transportation issues.

5: Easy Interface: This app is designed for simplicity and speed, making trading easy for everyone.

6: Various payment methods including cash on delivery.

7: Safe and reliable platform: Designed to ensure a safe environment and smooth, reliable transactions.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. At the start of the day, poultry farmers offer a corresponding quantity of fresh eggs from their hens for auction via this mobile app.
2. Verified buyers place bids.
3. The highest bid is accepted, and the app finalizes the transaction by confirming the price, quantity, and delivery terms, and sending a confirmation notification to both parties.
4. The app automatically forwards confirmed orders to registered transport companies so that the eggs can be picked up from the farm and delivered to the buyer as quickly as possible.
5. The buyer or their representative pays the transport company in cash and receives the eggs.
6. Poultry farmers adjust their offers based on the bids placed during the auction to reflect market developments.
7. Poultry farmers typically sell their eggs within two hours of the auction starting. The mobile app's trading hours are from 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
8. If the farmer does not accept any bids by the end of the auction, the offered batch of eggs will be automatically removed from the auction.
9. The transporter is obligated to deliver the eggs within six hours.
