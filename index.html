About Us

EggX is a digital marketplace for fresh eggs that connects poultry farmers across the country with a vast network of fresh egg consumers in their areas, where they can sell their farm eggs at the best prices they want.

Key Features:

1: Real-time Bids and Auctions: The app promotes market transparency and accurate price trends through real-time auction bids on offers to protect farmers and egg consumers from future losses.

2: Transparent Pricing: Monitor market trends and price changes to ensure fair and competitive transactions.

3: Verified Genuine Buyers::Opportunity to connect directly with verified consumers, eliminating the hassle of marketing.

4: Efficient Transaction Management: The app offers an automated logistics system so that farmers and buyers can continue their business activities without getting bogged down in transportation issues.

5: Easy Interface: This app is designed for simplicity and speed, making trading easy for everyone.

6: Various payment methods including cash on delivery.

7: Safe and reliable platform: Designed to ensure a safe environment and smooth, reliable transactions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Poultry farms offer their egg production for auction on this mobile application. On which pre-verified genuine buyers place bids based on their desired quantity. Through the continuous bids submitted during the auction, the egg sellers get an idea of ​​the market supply and demand and get the opportunity to make their decision based on this real price fluctuation. Usually, the seller chooses the highest bid during the two-hour period given for trading and closes the deal. If the seller does not accept any bids before the end of the auction time, the lot offered for auction is automatically removed from the trading window. Once the bid is accepted, the platform completes the transaction by confirming the agreed price, quantity, and delivery terms and notifies both parties of the deal confirmation. The buyer must immediately pay the price of the lot offered for auction to finalize the order. The method of payment for prices is separately specified in the application. Buyers who have chosen Cash on Delivery (COD) are exempt from advance payment and will pay in cash upon receipt of the goods. After the deal is completed, the platform consolidates multiple orders in the same area or route to ensure efficient transportation. In other words, small orders are sent with other orders in the area instead of being sent individually. The transporter waits for other orders until all the goods are equal to the load of a commercially viable vehicle, which reduces logistics costs and improves efficiency. After meeting this threshold, the platform sends a delivery order to the registered transporter, which contains details such as pickup and delivery locations, quantity, and quality. The seller has to prepare the goods and finalize the shipping arrangements within an hour of order confirmation, otherwise failure to meet this deadline will result in a penalty of Rs. 10,000 per shipment, which will be automatically deducted from the seller’s credit limit or collected from other sources. On the other hand, the transporter also has to pick up and deliver the goods within a strict time frame. All deliveries must be delivered within 5 hours of picking up the goods, otherwise the transporter will have to pay a penalty of Rs. 5,000 or more. The goods will be delivered to a pre-selected collection point, where the buyer or their representative will receive it. Throughout this entire transaction and logistics process, the platform keeps a digital record of all bids, transactions, payments, and logistics activities to maintain complete transparency. This application is designed to simplify and improve the trade of perishable goods, speed up delivery, ensure fair prices and freshness of products, as well as ensure market access for both buyers and sellers.
