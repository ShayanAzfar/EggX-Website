8: Order Processing: Poultry farmers and aggregators list their products on the platform, mentioning the quantity of stock available for sale, its asking price and other specifications.

Verified buyers, including retailers, supermarkets, wholesalers, distributors, bakeries, restaurants, canteens, and corporate clients, review seller offers and submit competitive bids based on required quantities, preferred delivery locations, and current market demand.

Once a bid match with a seller’s asking price, the platform automatically executes the trade by locking the transaction. Both parties are promptly notified to confirm the agreed price and quantity purchased.

Once a transaction is confirmed, the platform consolidates multiple confirmed orders within the same geographic route to optimize logistics efficiency. Rather than processing fragmented

shipments, orders are pooled until a commercially viable vehicle load is achieved, thereby minimizing transportation costs and improving operational efficiency.

Once the consolidated shipment threshold is met, the platform assigns the load to a registered transporter. The transporter receives detailed execution instructions, including pickup points, load quantities, routing sequence, and designated delivery locations for each buyer.
